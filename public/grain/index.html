The world is a cellular automaton split into 16×16 chunks. A chunk where nothing moved goes to sleep and costs nothing until a neighbour disturbs it. Turn on Show chunks to watch them wake.

1–9 elements, S spawner mode, [ ] brush size, Space pause, N step, V chunks, X clear. Right-click erases.

Pick an element and draw. Try fire on the plank, or acid on the sand pile.
